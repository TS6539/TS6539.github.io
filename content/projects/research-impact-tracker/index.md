**[Explore the Impact page →](/impact/)**

This tracker maps where my publications are being cited, using open bibliographic data from OpenAlex and Google Scholar. As of November 2026, it records 113 citing works from 93 institutions in 36 countries.

The Impact page presents the results as an animated world map with a year-by-year playback from 2024 to 2026 and a yearly breakdown of citations, alongside a record of peer-review service.

If you keep your own citation and review records, I am happy to share this tracker setup and compare workflows.
