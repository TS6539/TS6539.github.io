When a researcher writes with a generative AI tool, who owns the resulting text, and who should be declared its author? Building on the "AI ghostwriter" debate (Draxler et al., 2024), this interview study examines how doctoral students and faculty reason about the gap between the ownership they feel over AI-assisted writing and the authorship they would declare in practice, and what justifications they give for each position.

The study is currently in ethics review, and recruitment will open soon.
