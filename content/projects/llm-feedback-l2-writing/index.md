This project studies what second-language writers actually do with feedback generated by large language models: which suggestions they take up, which they resist, and how their texts and revision strategies change over the course of a writing task.

The study uses quantitative ethnography, combining epistemic network analysis (ENA) of writing-process data with close analysis of drafts and revisions, so that patterns of engagement with LLM feedback can be traced rather than only self-reported.

A pilot phase has been completed and was presented at ICQE 2026. The main study is currently in preparation.
