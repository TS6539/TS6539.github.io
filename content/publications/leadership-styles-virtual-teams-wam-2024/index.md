abstract: "This investigation examines the effects of globalization on the emergence of virtual teams and the optimal leadership approaches to support them. Beginning in the 1940s with the onset of globalization, there has been a notable increase in the use of virtual teams, primarily driven by advancements in technology and the quest for global talent. While these teams offer the benefits of flexibility and a variety of expertise, they also present challenges such as establishing trust and managing differences in time zones. This study adheres to PRISMA guidelines and thoroughly reviews 39 peer-reviewed articles from 2013-2023. The results primarily identify transformational leadership as the most common approach, with shared and transactional leadership also being significant. The success of these leadership styles depends on various factors, including trust, the use of technology, emotional intelligence, and quality of communication. The review suggests that no universal leadership style is suitable for all virtual teams, emphasizing the importance of adaptability and the ability to respond to specific situations."
