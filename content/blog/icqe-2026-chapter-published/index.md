Our paper, "Patterns of Generative AI Use Associated with Higher- and Lower-Quality Source-Based English Writing," co-authored with Paul McGachey and Marshall Higa, has been published as Chapter 16 of the ICQE 2026 proceedings (Springer, Communications in Computer and Information Science, vol. 3227, pp. 231-246).

The study uses quantitative ethnography to compare how higher- and lower-quality source-based English writing differed in the way students worked with ChatGPT during a timed writing task.

Read the chapter: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-44026-6_16
